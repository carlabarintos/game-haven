Game Haven Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account.
  • Your games, scores and settings stay on your iPhone or iPad.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some data to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • If you buy Remove Ads, no more ads are shown, and Apple handles the payment.

What stays on your device

Game progress, saved games, the village, and your settings (text size, contrast, sound and music) are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Until you buy Remove Ads, the app shows an occasional full-screen ad between games, never during a game. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see them, prevent fraud and measure results, Google's software may collect:

  • a device advertising identifier, but only if you allow tracking (see below);
  • your approximate location, worked out from your internet (IP) address;
  • information about your device and how you use ads, such as ads seen and tapped;
  • crash and performance information about the ad software.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Tracking: the app asks your permission before an advertising identifier is used. Saying no still gives you the same games and the same number of ads; they are just less personalised. You can change this any time in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example the UK and Europe), Google's consent form appears first. You can reopen it from the app's Settings screen with “Privacy settings”.
  • No ads at all: buy Remove Ads in the app's Settings screen.

Purchases

Remove Ads is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or Apple Account details. The app only checks with Apple whether Remove Ads has been bought.

Children

Game Haven is made for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
